Say it, check it, save it

A mic button on every “add” form (transactions, bills, debts, loans, and everything on the Plan page). You say it the way you'd tell a friend, the app fills in the form, and you check it before anything is saved. Free: the phone's own speech recognition hears you, and the app's own rules understand it (no AI service, nothing sent anywhere by us).

What you can say

  • Spent 250 on groceries yesterday → expense, ₱250, Groceries, yesterday
  • Got my salary 18,000 today → income, ₱18,000, Salary, today
  • Electric bill 2,400 due on the 15th every month → new bill, monthly, due the 15th
  • Paid the internet bill → marks that bill paid this month
  • I lent Ana 1,000 today → new loan (I lent), Ana, ₱1,000
  • Credit card 15,000, minimum 1,500 on the 20th → new debt
  • Put 500 in the travel pot → adds to that savings pot

Categories, bills, people and pots are matched to your names. If something isn't clear (no amount, an unknown category), that field stays empty and is highlighted for you to fill. Nothing is ever saved without you tapping Save.
