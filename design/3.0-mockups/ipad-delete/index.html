Today everything stretches across the whole iPad, so rows get very long. A centred column keeps the phone layout and leaves calm space at the sides (iPad Settings and Notes do the same).

Delete account inside the app

Opens from Settings › About & Help › Delete account. It says exactly what goes and what stays, asks for your password (that's also how the app proves it's you), and only then deletes. Your answer is built in: what you added to a shared household stays.
