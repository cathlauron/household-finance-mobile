Trips and events on the Calendar

Each phone shows the same month: a Cebu trip from Nov 12 to 17, and Mama's birthday on Nov 25. Each one plays Compact, then Details, then a tapped day. Pick the one you like, or tell me what to change.

A. A ribbon with the name I suggest this

One band runs across the whole trip with its name on it, and carries on to the next row. An event gets a small band too, though a one-day band cuts long names short. You can spot both without tapping, even in Compact.

B. Shaded days

The trip's days get a soft shade joined together, and an event's day is shaded too. It's the calmest look, but Compact shows no names, and a shaded event can look like today's circle.

C. A mark on every day

Each trip day gets the usual dot, or its name in Details. It's closest to today's Calendar, but the days don't look connected, and the name repeats on every day.

Works the same with any of them

  • A tapped day shows the trip as "Trip · day 2 of 6". Tapping that row opens the trip.
  • A trip with only a start date, or a one-day trip, shows on that one day.
  • A trip that runs into next month stops at the end of this month and carries on in the next. Greyed days stay empty, as they do now.
  • A trip or event with no amount never changes your projected balance.
