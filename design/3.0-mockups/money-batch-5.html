Different takes on change 1, plus the exchange rate you asked for on change 4. Names and amounts are samples. Nothing is built.

Option C: start from To-Pay instead

Nothing changes on Money in. The New loan and New debt forms get a switch: "I received this money". Turn it on and pick the account it went into.

BPI Savings goes up by ₱5,000, and it shows on Transactions as money in.

In every option: the money goes into the account you pick, it shows on Transactions, and it does not count as income. Removing that transaction within the undo time would ask whether to remove the loan or debt too. A and C can also be built together.
