What happens in the animation

  1. You tap 🎙 Say it on the widget.
  2. Finance Flow opens straight onto the listening sheet; you say "Spent 250 on groceries".
  3. You check it and tap Save (the form step is skipped in this animation).
  4. Back on the home screen the widget's Left to spend counts down to ₱12,230, the ring moves, Today becomes −₱630.
  5. The island grows for a moment: "Saved · Groceries −₱250", then tucks back.
Islands you'd see
  • Just saved (2-3 seconds): what you added and the new Left to spend.
  • Bill due today: "Electric ₱2,400 due today" with a Pay button when expanded.
  • Trip in progress (while a trip's dates are on): spent so far vs budget.

Good to know
  • Widgets and the island show amounts on your home screen and lock screen; a setting shows •••• instead (and "Hide amounts" in the app hides them too).
