Tapping anywhere else opens the app. Amounts respect "Hide amounts" (shown as ••••) and the app's lock still applies when it opens.

Good to know

  • Small: Left to spend + days to payday. Medium: plus the next bill and the two buttons. Large: plus bills due soon and today's spending.
  • Widgets refresh when you save something in the app (and every so often by themselves).
  • Voice works through the widget's Say it button: it opens the app on the listening sheet (a widget can't use the microphone itself).
  • Android first (it needs new phone-side code, so it comes with a build). iPhone widgets come with the iPhone version.
  • Privacy: the widget shows totals on your home screen. A setting lets you show •••• instead.
