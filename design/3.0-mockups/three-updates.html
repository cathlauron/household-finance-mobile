Each one shows what is on your Calendar: bills, debts, loans, paydays, trips and events. Tapping a day or a row opens that day in the app. Hidden amounts (the eye) show as ₱ ***** here too.

2. "Haven't logged anything today" reminder

Once a day, at a time you pick (8:00 PM suggested), but only if nothing was added today by anyone in the household. It has two buttons: one opens New transaction, the other says nothing was spent, which quiets it for today. It never comes on a day you already logged something.
