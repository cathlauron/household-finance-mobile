Peel-off switches

Every sticker peels from its top-right corner toward the bottom-left, and the corner follows your finger. While you peel, the screen already shows a peek of the setting, as much as you've peeled: Dark mode spreads in from the top-right corner of the screen, falling leaves drift in, mood colours warm the page, the glow and the rain fade in, and live weather shows its little forecast at the top.

  1. Let go before halfway: the sticker lies back down and the peek goes away. Nothing changes.
  2. Past halfway: it comes off the rest of the way, and the setting is on for real.
  3. Tap the sticker: it peels itself off at one steady speed (about a second), showing the same peek on the way.
  4. To turn it off: tap the green part. The sticker rolls back on at the same steady speed and the setting goes away with it.
  5. Remove animations on the phone: it just switches, no peel and no peek.

On the phone this runs on the animation thread like the tab swipe, with a small tick when it comes off.
