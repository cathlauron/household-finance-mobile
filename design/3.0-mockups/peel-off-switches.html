Peel-off switches

Put your finger (or the mouse) on a sticker and drag. The corner you're holding follows your finger: the sticker folds over along a line between where you started and where your finger is, its back side shows, and what's underneath (On, or Dark) shows through the gap.

  1. Let go before halfway: the sticker lies back down and nothing changes.
  2. Past halfway: it comes off the rest of the way by itself, and the setting is on. Dark mode really switches this mock to dark.
  3. To turn it off: tap the green part (or "Dark"). The sticker rolls back on.
  4. Tapping the sticker without pulling peels it off too, for anyone who'd rather tap.
  5. Remove animations on the phone: it simply switches, no peeling.

On the phone this runs on the animation thread (like the tab swipe), with a small tick when it comes off. Pull from either right-hand corner: grab the top half to peel the top corner, the bottom half for the bottom one.
