Finance Flow

Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Finance Flow is a household budgeting app. This page explains, in plain words, what it keeps, where, who can see it, and how to delete it.

The short version. Your money data is locked (encrypted) on your phone before it is saved online, so only you and the people in your household can read it. Finance Flow has no ads, does not sell your data, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

What Finance Flow keeps

  • Your account: your email address and the username you choose. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; Finance Flow never sees it in plain text.
  • Your money data: everything you type in (accounts, transactions, bills, debts, loans, plans, notes) and any photos you add (a profile picture or receipts). This is encrypted on your phone before it is saved online.
  • What's needed to run the app: which household you belong to, who added or changed an item, the phones you're signed in on (the phone's name and when it was last used, so you can sign other phones out), and short-lived invite and recovery codes. Your password-protected keys are stored too, but they are themselves locked.
  • Settings on your phone: your look, reminders and similar choices stay on your phone.

Location, camera and photos

  • Location is only used if you turn on Live weather. Your location is rounded to about 1 km, kept on your phone, and sent only to the weather service Open-Meteo to match the sky on Home to your sunrise, sunset and weather. Your money data is never sent with it.
  • Camera is only used to scan a household invite code.
  • Photos are only read when you choose one (a profile picture or a receipt).
  • Reminders are set up on your phone itself; no reminder content is sent to a server.

Who can see your data

You and the people you invite into your household. Up to 5 people can share one household, and anything anyone adds shows up for everyone in it. Finance Flow does not share or sell your data to anyone else.

Services Finance Flow uses

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Hosting) stores your account and your encrypted data, and hosts these web pages.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather, only if Live weather is on (see above).

How long data is kept

Your data is kept while you have an account. When you ask to delete your account, your account and your data are deleted within 30 days. If you share a household, what the other members added stays with them.

Your choices

  • Turn Live weather off at any time in the app’s Appearance settings.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by emailing support.
  • Delete your account: see how to delete your account.

Children

Finance Flow is meant for adults managing a household. It is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep data about them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.

Contact
