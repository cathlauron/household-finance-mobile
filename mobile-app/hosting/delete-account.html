Finance Flow

Delete your Finance Flow account

In the app: Settings › About & Help › Delete account. Without the app, you can ask us here at any time.

How to ask

  1. Email support.financeflow00@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with.
  2. Use the subject Delete my account and include your Finance Flow username.
  3. We reply to confirm it's you, then delete your account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

Email support now

Never send your password or your Recovery Key. We will never ask for them.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email and username) and your sign-in on every phone.
  • Your money data and photos, if you are the only person in your household.
  • Your Recovery Key and your password-protected keys, and any invite or recovery codes.

If you share a household

You leave the household. What you added to the shared budget stays there for the other members, because it was added before your account was deleted.

What we keep

Nothing from your account after it is deleted. Emails you sent to support stay in our support inbox.
